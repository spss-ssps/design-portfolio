01 — Brand Identity

Wavelength

A full rebrand for an independent streaming platform — identity system, motion logo, and a typographic language built to hold up across a thousand thumbnails.

IdentityMotionType System

The Brief

Wavelength needed a rebrand that could survive a thousand tiny thumbnails and still feel unmistakably theirs on a billboard. The old mark was three years old and had never been drawn as a system — every team was recreating it by eye.

The Approach

I built a modular wordmark that flexes across a proportional grid, paired with a motion signature derived from actual audio waveforms, so the brand literally moves to the content it's sitting on.

02 — Product Design

Terra

End-to-end UX for a sustainable goods marketplace — research, IA, and a component system that shipped across web and native without losing its voice.

UI/UXDesign SystemMobile

The Brief

Terra was scaling fast across web and native with two separate design languages fighting each other. The team needed one system, one source of truth, and a UX that could hold hundreds of SKUs without feeling like a spreadsheet.

The Approach

I ran discovery with the support and merchandising teams, restructured the IA around how people actually shop sustainably, and shipped a component library that both platforms now build from directly.

  • RoleProduct Designer
  • Timeline4 months
  • DeliverablesResearch, IA, design system

Nocturne
Layer / Campaign
03 — Visual + Motion

Adjacent Issue 12: Archive

Designed and Drafted 9 posts, one for each contributor in Adjacent’s Issue 12, and maintained a cohesive brand identity throughout by consistently using a black-and-white color palette, while ensuring each individual project remained the focal point of its respective post.

Graphic DesignMotionPrint

The Brief

Nocturne needed a visual language flexible enough to work across a touring poster series, a projection package, and social — without designing three separate campaigns from scratch each stop.

The Approach

I designed a generative poster system seeded by each city's own sound data, paired with a looping projection piece that venues could run live behind the DJs each night.
